The Story of Tally Simple

Querying Tally ERP / Prime directly over HTTP (port 9000) requires complex XML request wrappers and obscure TDL syntax. tally-simple acts as the pure transport engine: choose an endpoint, supply a company name, and receive Tally's exact, raw XML response.

Following the UNIX philosophy, responses are kept as raw XML text—making them 100% loss-free, directly pipeable into shell files (> units.xml), and ideal for downstream transformation layers like tally-simple-json.
